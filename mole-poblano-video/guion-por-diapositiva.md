**Diapositiva 1 · Cover**

> Hello! My name is [YOUR NAME], and today I'm going to share my favorite dish in the world: mole poblano.

**Diapositiva 2 · Dark · Rich · Sweet · Spicy**

> It's dark, rich, a little sweet and a little spicy, and every bite tastes like home.

**Diapositiva 3 · Puebla, Mexico**

> Mole poblano comes from Puebla, Mexico. The legend says that nuns created it in the seventeenth century, mixing chiles, spices and chocolate to welcome an archbishop. Today, we serve it at weddings, birthdays and big family celebrations.

**Diapositiva 4 · Ingredients 1/5 · Protein**

> Let's start with the ingredients. For eight people, you need one and a half kilos of chicken or turkey.

**Diapositiva 5 · Ingredients 2/5 · Dried chiles**

> For the sauce: three kinds of dried chiles, ancho, mulato and pasilla.

**Diapositiva 6 · Ingredients 3/5 · Nuts & seeds**

> Then almonds, peanuts, sesame seeds and raisins.

**Diapositiva 7 · Ingredients 4/5 · Fresh & more**

> A plantain, two tomatoes, onion, garlic and a piece of bread.

**Diapositiva 8 · Ingredients 5/5 · Spices & chocolate**

> Add cinnamon, cloves, pepper and anise. And finally, a little Mexican chocolate.

**Diapositiva 9 · Step 1 · Boil the chicken**

> Step one: boil the chicken with onion, garlic and salt for thirty minutes. Keep that broth, you'll need it later.

**Diapositiva 10 · Step 2 · Toast the chiles**

> Step two: toast the chiles for just a few seconds on each side. Careful! If they burn, your mole will be bitter. Remove the seeds and soak the chiles in hot water for twenty minutes.

**Diapositiva 11 · Step 3 · Fry**

> Step three: fry the nuts, seeds, spices, plantain, bread, tomato, onion and garlic until golden.

**Diapositiva 12 · Step 4 · Blend**

> Step four: blend everything with the chiles and a little broth, until it's a smooth paste.

**Diapositiva 13 · Step 5 · Fry the paste & simmer**

> Step five: fry that paste in hot oil for ten minutes. Watch out, it splatters! Then add the broth, the chocolate, a little sugar and salt, and simmer for one hour, stirring often.

**Diapositiva 14 · Step 6 · Add the chicken**

> Finally, add the chicken and cook ten more minutes.

**Diapositiva 15 · Serve with**

> Serve it with rice, warm tortillas and sesame seeds on top. The recipe takes about three hours, so cook it on a relaxed day. My tip: mole is even better the next day, so make extra!

**Diapositiva 16 · Thanks for watching!**

> Mole takes time, patience and love, but sharing it is always worth it. Thanks for watching, and buen provecho!
